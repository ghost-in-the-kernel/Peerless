Solutions That Overcome Cyber Challenges

Peerless is an SBA-certified business offering cyber solutions and experience to Federal clients to meet their mission-critical information, software, network, and hardware vulnerability challenges.

We are an award-winning business supporting:

  • DoD aeronautical system assurance requirements
  • Defense Information Assurance Certification and Accreditation (DIACAP)
  • Cyber threat analysis
  • Security performance auditing

Peerless’ growing cyber practice contains the tools, applications, and cyber capabilities to protect the cyberspace of our clients.

We are experienced in providing clients with a cleared and qualified cyber workforce, including Information Assurance (IA) officers equipped with IA and systems security qualifications, to overcome cyber challenges. Our cyber practice includes intrusion avoidance, information and infrastructure assurance, and assessments on hacker malware strategies on vulnerable Internet-facing websites.

Capabilities and Experience

  • Information Assurance
  • Cyber and Information Assurance Policy Planning and Documentation
  • Network Security Monitoring and Analysis
  • Security Operations Center Support

  • Security Performance Auditing
  • Security Awareness Program Development and Training
