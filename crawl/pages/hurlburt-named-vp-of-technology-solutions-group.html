Hurlburt named VP of Technology & Solutions group

ByMike Wallace July 5, 2022

Peerless Technologies Corporation has named David (Dave) Hurlburt, a technology and business leader, as Vice President of its Technology & Solutions group.

“Dave’s extensive background in R&D and engineering make him a strong addition to the Peerless team,” said Peerless President Andrea Kunk. “As we position Peerless for the next phase of its growth, Dave’s capabilities strengthen our ability to bring robust technical services and solutions to current and future customers.“

As VP of the Technology & Solutions group, Hurlburt will expand the company’s reach into advanced research and engineering programs, as well as oversee ongoing work supporting the Air Force Nuclear Weapons Center, the Air Force Small Business Innovation Research/Small Business Technology Transfer program and more.

Hurlburt joins Peerless from Leidos, where he spent more than 10 years overseeing engineering and R&D programs for AFRL, the Air Force Life Cycle Management Center, DARPA and other agencies. He has also worked at other IT, defense, and engineering companies.

“I’m very excited to join Peerless Technologies,” Hurlburt said. “Peerless has a stellar and noteworthy history, a fantastic reputation, and an exciting future trajectory.”

Hurlburt holds a BSE in Nuclear Engineering from the University of Michigan and an MS in Nuclear Engineering & Physics from North Carolina State University.

About Peerless Technologies Corporation

Peerless, founded in 2000 and based in Dayton, Ohio, specializes in complex professional services, including systems engineering, cybersecurity, program management, research and development, test and evaluation, and financial, logistics and business management. Peerless clients include NASA’s Armstrong Flight Research Center; The F-22 Program Office; the Air Force Nuclear Weapons Center, Air Force Civil Engineer Center, the Air Force Small Business Innovation Research program, the F-35 Joint Program Office, and other federal agencies.
