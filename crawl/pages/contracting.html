Contract with Peerless for Cost-Effective Services and Engineering Solutions

We offer many vehicles to make contracting with us — including our two small business Joint Ventures — easy, fast and economical.

Peerless contracting offers you:

  • Contract execution experience with more than $407 million in GSA contracts.
  • Procurement time savings using our vehicles that offer pre-negotiated service rates, verified capabilities, and vetted past performance.
  • Step-by-step instruction and recommendations from Mindy Waldren, VP of Contracts & Pricing, and her prime and subcontract, and pricing expert team. Contact Waldren at 937-490-5006 for answers to your GSA Schedule questions.

Peerless Prime Contract Vehicles

GSA Multiple Award Schedule – Professional Services Category (MAS-PSC)

Peerless offers our GSA MAS-PSC to give our federal customers a more streamlined approach to procuring professional financial, business, logistics and engineering solutions with multiple NAICS codes, while offering shorter lead times, lower administrative costs, and more flexibility.

Explore MAS-PSC
GSA OASIS Small Business and Unrestricted IDIQ Contracts

Our multiple GSA One Acquisition Solution for Integrated Services (OASIS) IDIQ contract vehicles in both the Unrestricted (Pool 1) and Small Business (Pools 4 and 6) provide flexible and innovative solutions for complex professional services such as program management services, management consulting services, logistics services, engineering services, scientific services, and financial services.

We also offer our SierTeK-Peerless Joint Venture OASIS Small Business Pools 1, 1-8(a), 3, 3-8(a) with their manifold NAICS, as well as their 8(a) designations, which makes getting to Peerless quicker and easier.

Explore Peerless’ OASIS Unrestricted and Small Business IDIQ contracts
SeaPort-Next Generation (Seaport-NxG)

Peerless offers our Navy warfighting communities our Seaport-Next Generation IDIQ vehicle awarded to our BQMI-Peerless Joint Venture, which is a certified 8(a), woman-owned small business joint venture eligible to bid and win Seaport-NxG small business set-aside contracts in the engineering, acquisition, business, and IT domains.

Explore SeaPort-Next Generation
Defense Information Systems Agency (DISA) ENCORE III Small Business

DISA awarded the SierTeK-Peerless Joint Venture an ENCORE III Small Business IDIQ vehicle to support DoD Cybersecurity, Enterprise IT, Cloud professional services, integrated solutions, and test and evaluation.

Responsive Strategic Sourcing for Services (RS3)

Peerless support to the Army, Navy, and Air Force is well suited for RS3, a Small Business IDIQ vehicle that provides support services for programs with Command, Control, Communications, Computers, Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance related requirements to include:

  • Engineering & Systems Engineering and Technical Assistance (SETA)
  • Research, Development, Test and Evaluation
  • Logistics
  • Acquisition and Strategic Planning
  • Education and Training

Customers may include various Army Contracting Command – Aberdeen Proving Ground), Program Executive Offices; other Department of Defense agencies, and other federal agencies with C4ISR-related requirements.

Explore Responsive Strategic Sourcing for Services
NASA Pace IV

Peerless Technologies Corporation (Peerless) provides high quality products and services in information technology, information management, and multimedia to the NASA Glenn Research Center (GRC) at Lewis Field, Cleveland, OH, and its associated sites and facilities (Plum Brook Station, Sandusky, OH), and other areas that result in a high level of customer satisfaction. Our PACE IV personnel perform technical tasks to assist the GRC in meeting the objectives of its research, development, engineering, and institutional support activities.
