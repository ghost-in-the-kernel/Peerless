About Peerless

Peerless Technologies Corporation, founded in 2000, offers diverse capabilities of cybersecurity and cyber defense operations, enterprise IT, lifecycle systems engineering, program support and operations support. With over 400 employees nationwide, Peerless performs a wide array of cybersecurity, IT, software development, program management services, operations support and other complex professional services for NASA, U.S Air Force, Navy and other federal clients. Our work includes supporting the F-22 Program Office, the Air Force Distributed Common Ground System; Air Force Nuclear Weapons Center; Naval Surface Warfare Center Crane Division, NASA centers in Ohio, Florida and California; and much more.
 
Headquartered in Dayton, Ohio, Peerless has employees in 27 states, Europe and Africa.

Core Values

People

Our priority is to provide customers with total satisfaction by turning their requirements into quality products and services. We work hard to foster our employees’ growth, because they are essential to our success! We provide employees opportunities for career challenges, training, and development.

Quality

Meeting or exceeding customer requirements is just one of the ways we cultivate a culture of quality. We ensure services are performed as requested, and deliverables are submitted on time. We also monitor and measure performance of key corporate functions and processes.

Integrity

We always operate with integrity by meeting high ethical standards and embracing the Peerless Ethics Policy. We willingly collaborate and remain loyal to one another as a team.

Value

We create the best value for our customers by using fair and balanced billing rates, competitive employee compensation, and overall pricing. We carefully manage our business practices, hold each other accountable, and deliver everything better than our competition to accomplish our mission.

Our Vision

To deliver cost-effective professional services and engineering solutions that drive mission success.

What We Offer

Peerless Technologies provides a wide range of contract vehicles, ensuring seamless support for mission-critical needs across defense, space, energy, and security sectors. We work with federal agencies, including the Department of Defense, NASA, DOE, EPA, and DHS.

Our Services Include:

  • Audited Systems: DCAA-compliant accounting and DCMA-approved purchasing systems ensure transparency and accountability.
  • Talent Solutions: Comprehensive recruiting, staffing, and HR management for tailored workforce solutions.
  • Security Oversight: Facility Security Officer (FSO) oversight, ensuring secure and compliant operations.
  • We deliver flexible, responsive, and proven solutions that help our clients achieve their mission goals.

Awards & Facilities

Peerless is a multi-year winner of the Dayton Business Journal’s Best Places to Work Award. We have been named one of the Dayton Metro Area’s Top Workplaces for multiple years by the Dayton Daily News, and were named to the Inc. 5000 List of Fastest Growing Companies in America in 2013.

Our state-of- the-art business facility in Fairborn, Ohio, includes 1,500 square feet of SCIF-ready space and is available to our customers for meetings, teleconferences, and mission support. We can access facilities and infrastructure to accommodate immediate project expansion within 48 hours for new project personnel.
