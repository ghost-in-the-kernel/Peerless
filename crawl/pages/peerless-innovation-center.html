The Peerless Innovation Center (PIC) serves as the company’s S&T center of excellence

Leveraging 20+ years of customer-first engineering services, the Peerless Innovation Center aims to advance high-end R&D technologies and generate differentiating capabilities for Peerless and their customers through research, development, and execution of cutting-edge technology programs.

The PIC primarily performs externally-funded contract research and development (CRAD) work for customers in the Defense, Energy and Space S&T organizations.

Core capabilities include:

Multi-spectral Sensor Engineering
  • Electronic Warfare
  • Electro-optical/Infrared (EO/IR)
  • Pod/Platform Integration/modernization (fifth-generation fighters)
Modeling & Simulation
  • Engineering/Physics models
  • Mission-level Modeling, Simulation & Analysis
Digital System Engineering & Transformation
  • Software Modernization
  • Digital Systems Engineering/Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) processes, tools, and Digital Thread
  • MBSE to auto-generate flight test plans for NASA autonomy testing
Software Engineering/DevSecOps
  • Micro-service in a Service Oriented Architecture (SOA)
  • Agile CI/CD
  • Collaborative workspace dev environment
  • Production Env Mirror
  • C, C++, Java, FORTRAN, COBOL, JavaScript, Python, LabVIEW, Data Access Language, Structured Query Language (SQL), Visual Basic, and VBScript MATLAB, LabVIEW, Oracle’s Virtual Box, Abaqus; and operating systems: Linux/Unix, Macintosh, Microsoft, and RHEL clone
  • Automated regression tests, automated acceptance testing, and human-in-the-loop testing
Airworthiness
  • Independent airworthiness assessments
  • Experimental structures R&D, including thermal, mechanical, and acoustic/vibration conditions
Nuclear Sustainment & Effects
  • Modeling & Simulation of nuclear effects
  • Test & Evaluation support
  • Stockpile modernization
  • Systems engineering technical support, aircraft integration, test and evaluation, logistics, risk management, T&E logistics
