Andrea Kunk

Andrea Kunk

President

Andrea Kunk, a charter employee of Peerless Technologies, has served as President since 2021. She joined Peerless in 2000 as its second employee and has helped shepherd the company’s growth to more than 500 employees in 21 states and more than $100 million in annual revenue.

Kunk has more than 20 years of management and technology experience, principally in the defense industry. She is a past winner of the Dayton Business Journal’s Forty under 40 and was a past finalist for the DBJ’s Small Business CFO of the Year award.

Kunk holds an M.B.A. (Finance) and a B.S. in Management Information Systems, both from Wright State University. She is the Chair of the Wright State University Foundation Board of Trustees and also serves as the Vice President of the Fairborn Development Corporation Board of Directors. She is based in Ohio.
