Cost-Effective Professional and Engineering Solutions

Peerless delivers Systems Engineering, Cybersecurity, IT, Human and Medical Performance, Innovation and Research support to Defense, Space, Energy, and Homeland Security clients. We provide various GSA schedules and contract vehicles for easy customer access.

Quality Management and Advanced Facility Capabilities

We have implemented an ISO 9001:2015 certified Quality Management System and CMMI Level 3 software and systems engineering processes to ensure quality deliverables and repeatable successes. Our state-of- the-art facility is available to our customers for meetings and teleconferences. We also have facilities and infrastructure to accommodate new project personnel within 48 hours of contract award.

Comprehensive Advisory and Assistance Support (A&AS)

Peerless provides Advisory and Assistance Support (A&AS) within all our areas of expertise. This support includes financial management, accounting and budgeting services, Earned Value Management (EVM), program and project management (PMI credentials), risk management (using industry best practices and tools), cost and schedule analysis, integrated master scheduling, BRAC support, policy compliance, and acquisition planning.

Our Services

Peerless provides comprehensive services across various sectors, including Aerospace, Cybersecurity, Energy Conservation, Information Technology, and Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance (ISR). Our expertise extends to Medical and Human Performance, Program Management, Research and Development, Test and Evaluation, Systems Engineering, and Technology Innovation Support (SBIR/STTR). Additionally, we offer advanced Training and Training Systems to ensure our clients receive top-tier solutions tailored to their mission-critical needs.
